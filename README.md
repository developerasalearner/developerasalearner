## 🎯 Research Vision

I believe that **rigorous machine learning research must be reproducible, transparent, and accessible**. This principle guides my work:

- **Interpretability First:** Developing algorithms that are not only accurate but also understandable and trustworthy
- **Open Science:** Publishing code, datasets, and methodologies openly for community validation and advancement
- **Practical Impact:** Bridging the gap between theoretical advances and real-world applications
- **Continuous Learning:** Staying current with research literature while maintaining deep expertise in core areas

My long-term goal is to contribute fundamental advances in machine learning—particularly in pattern recognition, metric learning, and explainable AI—while building a community around reproducible research practices.
